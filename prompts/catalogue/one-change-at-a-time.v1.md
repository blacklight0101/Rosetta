## Rule

Measure the build with a few fixed measures and make one change to the process at a time, then compare.

## Why it matters

When several things change together nobody can tell which one helped; a few steady numbers show whether a change to the process paid off.

## How to check

The build method names its measures (first-time review passes, review rounds, cost per card, escaped defects), says where they are recorded, and each process change is compared with the numbers before it.
