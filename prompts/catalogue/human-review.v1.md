## Rule

Have a person review and approve every pull request, whoever or whatever wrote it.

## Why it matters

Agents produce plausible code quickly, including plausible mistakes; a human reviewer stays accountable for
what reaches the main branch.

## How to check

The main branch requires an approving review from a person; the review checklist covers the chosen principles;
no agent can merge on its own.
