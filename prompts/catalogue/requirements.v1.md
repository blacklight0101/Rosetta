## Rule

State each requirement as a user story with Given/When/Then scenarios and a MoSCoW priority.

## Why it matters

Scenarios turn vague wishes into examples that can be tested and agreed; the priority makes clear what can
move when time runs short.

## How to check

Every requirement has at least one scenario and one of Must, Should, Could or Won't; each scenario has a test
named after the requirement id.
