## Rule

Emit structured logs, metrics and traces so the running system can explain what it is doing.

## Why it matters

Problems in production are found and understood from evidence rather than guesswork, and the team learns how
the system is really used.

## How to check

Each request carries a correlation id through logs and traces; key business and technical metrics have
dashboards and alerts; logs carry no secrets or personal data.
