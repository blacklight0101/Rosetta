## Rule

Give every task card a risk class (security boundary, data loss, money or none) next to its difficulty.

## Why it matters

A small card at a trust boundary can do the most harm, so size alone is the wrong way to choose who builds it and how it is reviewed.

## How to check

Every card states its risk class, and a card with a risk class other than none is built by at least the middle model tier and reviewed with its probes.
