## Rule

Track deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate and time to restore service.

## Why it matters

These four measures show whether delivery is getting faster and safer, and point at the bottleneck instead of
relying on impressions.

## How to check

The four metrics are collected from the pipeline and incident records and reviewed at a regular cadence with a
target for each.
