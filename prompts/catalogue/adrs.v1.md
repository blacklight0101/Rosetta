## Rule

Record every decision that is costly to reverse as a short ADR with its context, options, choice and consequences.

## Why it matters

Months later the team and newcomers can see why the system is built this way, and a decision can be revisited
knowingly instead of by accident.

## How to check

Each significant choice in the package has an ADR with at least two options considered; ADRs are never
deleted, only superseded or amended with a date.
