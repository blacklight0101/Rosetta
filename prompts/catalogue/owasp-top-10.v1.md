## Rule

Check every web feature against the current OWASP Top 10 list of the most common web application risks.

## Why it matters

Most real attacks exploit a handful of well-known weaknesses such as broken access control, injection and
misconfiguration.

## How to check

The review checklist maps each OWASP category to the controls in place; automated scans and tests cover access
control, injection and security headers.
