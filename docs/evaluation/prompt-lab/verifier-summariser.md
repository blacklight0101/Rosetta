## Measures, and the cheapest wrong answers each one stops

1. **Label agreement** per case (the existing `expected` comparison), now with `Disagreements (expected -> got)` in the summary and `--compare` (RF-1411, `lab-disagreements.ts`). It reports false "supported" (`Rejected -> Supported`), false "not supported" (`Supported -> Rejected`) and `-> Unverified` apart. It adds no per-label count: #1411 is merged, so the report prints its `Results by label:` line and the disagreements line beside it.
   - Everything `Supported`: agreement 50%, `Rejected -> Supported x15`.
   - Everything `Rejected`: agreement 50%, `Supported -> Rejected x15`.
   - No verdict (`I cannot tell.`): agreement 0%, every case `-> Unverified`, none usable.
   - Only an answer that follows the hand label reaches 100% with no disagreement. A test runs each of these over the real 30 cases through the real target (`lab-targets-verifier-calibration.test.ts`).
   - Two versions with the same agreement but different kinds of error show different pairs (`lab-disagreements.test.ts`).
2. **Merge labels** (RF-1412, `merge-check.ts`): `Invalid` (fewer than two ids, an unlisted id, an id twice), `Lumped` (a merge of more than half of a case of four cards or more), `Unrelated` (the cards of one merge are not linked by shared title words of four letters or more, added in the fix round after a probe that merged the first and the last card under a title naming one word of each and read `Merges`), `Thin` (the merged title and summary share no word of four letters with the title of one of its cards), `Merges`, `NoMerge`. Wrong answers tried: one merge of every card, a merge of one id, of an unlisted id, a merge of the first and the last card under a title naming one word of each, a merge whose text names one card only, text `Nothing to merge.`: each is not usable (`lab-targets-summariser.test.ts`, `merge-check.test.ts`). The merged card keeps every claim and citation by the run's own code, so the check guards what the answer can still lose: the ids, the lump, the relatedness and the text. Each case records `counts { merges, mergedIds, cards }`, so the merged ids are counted, not the answers.
3. **Overview labels** (RF-1412, `overview-check.ts`): `Empty` (no statement with text and a listed card, the run's `sectionsOf` rule), `Lumped` (one statement links every card), `Missed` (an area with no card linked, or a FEAT, SCRN, ENT or INT card whose section holds no statement linking a card of that type), `Written`. Wrong answers tried: all sections empty, a statement of only unlisted ids, one statement for every card, an overview with an area or a section left out: each is not usable. **These are guards, not a measure of quality**: an overview that copies the card titles, or writes one statement per section, reads `Written`, and the judge is the only check behind them.
4. The judge (Opus 5.5; trusted for the verifier family, "in the middle" for the summariser, `judge-sample.md`).

**What the measures cannot do.** `NoMerge` is a valid answer and is not punished by a label, because a case may hold no duplicate; a prompt that always answers `{"merges": []}` would pass the checks. The `Merges` count, the merged ids and `Unrelated` are a floor only: the merge cases have no hand labels, so the lab cannot tell a right merge from a wrong one, and under DEC-238 more merges are never a quality gain until hand labels exist (RF-1412). A label set written by hand is the open item.

## `verifier/retry` (wired in the fix round, RF-1413)
